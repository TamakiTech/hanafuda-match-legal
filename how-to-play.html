How to play

Flip a card, remember where it went, and find the others from the same month. The pictures do not have to match. January’s crane matches January’s pine. March’s curtain matches March’s blossoms.

Match 2 · Beginner

Turn two cards from the same month. A month on the table may be a pair, or all four of its cards. When four are dealt, any two of that month are a match, and the other two still match each other.

Match 3 · Intermediate

Find three cards from the same month. Every month on the table has three cards.

Match 4 · Expert

Find all four cards from a month. Every month on the table is complete.

A turn

A card from a different month ends that try at once. The cards turn face down again after a short look. A move is one completed try.

Matched cards leave their places empty. The rest of the grid stays where it is.

The board

Choose a grid that fits the screen. The board scales to the window and does not scroll. The clock starts when you flip the first card. Your best time for that mode and grid stays on this device.

The months shows one card from each of the twelve flowers, so you can learn which pictures belong together.
