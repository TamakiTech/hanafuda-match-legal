Privacy Policy

Hanafuda Match by Tamaki is a memory game. It does not create accounts, and it does not sell information about you.

Play stays on this device. Your best times, the mode and grid you last chose, and the mute setting are stored only on this phone or computer so the next sitting can remember them. The game does not send those scores anywhere.

There are no online rooms, no ads, and no advertising ID. The app does not use analytics.

When the device is online, the app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google may receive the usual connection data for that request, such as an IP address. A game still runs if that request cannot be made. The type then falls back to fonts already on the device.

You can delete the saved times and settings by clearing this app’s storage.

Hanafuda Match is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Questions: support@tamakitech.com. This policy is for Hanafuda Match 1.0.
